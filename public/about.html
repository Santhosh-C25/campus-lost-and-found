About Campus Lost & Found

A project built to make campus life a little less stressful.

Our Mission

The Campus Lost & Found Management System was created as part of the Advance Web Technology course to solve a real problem: the difficulty students face when they lose or find belongings on campus.

Our goal is to provide a simple, transparent, and safe platform where students can report lost and found items, submit claims, and coordinate returns with admin oversight.

🎯

Problem Statement

Students frequently lose items on campus. Without a central system, lost items often go unclaimed, causing unnecessary stress and financial loss.

💡

Our Solution

A web-based platform that connects students who have lost items with those who have found them, with administrator oversight for claim verification.

🔒

Safety First

All claims are reviewed by administrators before items are returned. User accounts are secured with bcrypt hashing and session-based authentication.

🛠️

Technology

Built with Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and vanilla HTML/CSS/JS as part of an Advance Web Technology curriculum project.

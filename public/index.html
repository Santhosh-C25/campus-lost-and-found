How It Works

Getting your belongings back is simple and secure.

📝

1. Report

Create a report for an item you lost or found on campus with details and a photo.

🔍

2. Search

Browse and filter reports by category, location, and date to find matching items.

✉️

3. Claim

Submit a claim with proof of ownership so the admin can verify and connect you.

🎉

4. Recover

Collect your item once the claim is approved. Item marked as resolved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a lost item?

Log in to your account, click "Report Lost Item" from the navigation, fill in the details, and submit. Your report will be visible to all users immediately.

How do I claim a found item?

Find the item in the listings, open its detail page, and click "Submit a Claim". Provide a detailed explanation proving ownership. An admin will review and approve or reject the claim.

How long does claim review take?

Administrators aim to review claims within 1–2 business days. You will see the status update in your "My Claims" page.

Can I edit a report after submitting?

Yes, you can edit your own reports as long as they are not yet resolved. Go to "My Reports" and click "Edit" next to the report.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Your contact details are only shown to administrators during claim review.
